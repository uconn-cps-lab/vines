Motivation & Research Foundations

The NextG Smart Manufacturing Challenge

Problem to Solve

A central challenge in Next-Generation Manufacturing Systems (NGMS) is ensuring resilient and deterministic connectivity across wired and wireless domains in dynamic, failure-prone shop floors.

Existing solutions are largely reactive and siloed, leaving manufacturing systems vulnerable to unpredictable interference, equipment failures, and other disruptions.

Project Contributions

This project develops an end-to-end architecture that integrates intelligent 5G and 6G devices with deterministic core networks based on Time-Sensitive Networking (TSN). It also develops a unified resource management framework that combines predictive intelligence at the wireless edge with proactive reconfiguration in the core network to help manufacturing systems remain safe, reliable, and efficient under adverse conditions.

Resilience & Convergence

Vector A

  • Resilience: Maintain continuous mission-critical operation despite severe radio interference, link degradation, or physical hardware faults.
  • Convergence: Coordinate seamlessly across heterogeneous wired (TSN) and wireless (5G/6G) domains with strict bounded latency and predictable performance.
AI-Native Design & ISAC

Vector B

  • AI-Native Design: Machine learning-driven, short-horizon prediction at the network edge to support timely, reliable proactive scheduling adaptation.
  • ISAC Integration: Incorporate Integrated Sensing and Communication into network awareness to detect physical obstacles before wireless dropouts occur.
Cross-Domain Decision Support

Convergence

  • The enabling technologies supply information and decision support to realize NGMS capabilities through short-horizon prediction.
  • Proactive edge/core adaptation and contract-based end-to-end integration establish verifiable SLA guarantees for industrial control loops.
System Architecture

The Three Interconnected Substrates

A vertically integrated architecture bridging high-level manufacturing intelligence, converged cyber networking, and physical industrial hardware.

📊

Monitoring & Control

Closed-loop real-time feedback with sub-millisecond control loops for CNC machines, robotic arms, and high-precision assembly tooling.

🩺

Diagnosis & Prognosis

Edge-assisted acoustic, thermal, and vibration anomaly monitoring to predict mechanical failure before catastrophic production stoppage.

👁️

Machine Vision

Ultra-high-definition multi-camera defect inspection streams requiring deterministic throughput and low-jitter packet ingestion.

🤖

Cobot Path Planning

Dynamic trajectory re-computation for collaborative robots interacting safely alongside human operators in shared factory workspaces.

📡

Predictive Wireless Edge

5G/6G URLLC gNodeB with cycle-aligned scheduling and ML-based channel prediction that anticipates RF fading up to dozens of milliseconds ahead.

⚡

Deterministic TSN Core

IEEE 802.1Qbv Time-Aware Shaper (TAS) switches, shadow schedule generation, and zero-touch failover guaranteeing microsecond jitter bounds under link failures.

🔄

Cross-Domain Control Layer

Jointly partitions, reserves, and orchestrates wireless resource blocks and TSN time slots to enforce end-to-end contracts and bounded latency.

🛡️

Zero-Touch Resilience

Pre-computed shadow schedules and automated re-routing mechanisms that guarantee zero packet loss for safety-critical streams during faults.

🦾

Collaborative Robots (Cobots)

Mobile and stationary cobots executing coordinated multi-arm material handling, assembly, welding, and quality validation tasks.

🏭

Dynamic Shop Floor

A complex physical terrain populated by moving AMRs, forklifts, human operators, and large reflective metal machinery causing multi-path fading.

📻

Dense IIoT Sensor Mesh

Heterogeneous sensor nodes communicating via configured grant 5G and industrial Ethernet to continuously stream environmental telemetry.

⚙️

Flexible Manufacturing Cells

Reconfigurable high-mix low-volume production stations capable of adapting workflows on demand without physical recabling.

Core Technical Pillars

The Three Integrated Research Thrusts

Establishing a closed-loop framework that anticipates risks, mitigates disruptions, safeguards critical data flows, and continuously refines resource allocation.

1
Wireless Edge Thrust

ML-Driven Perception, ISAC & Cycle-Aligned Edge Scheduling

Focuses on the wireless edge by developing machine learning-driven prediction mechanisms, Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) techniques, and cycle-aligned scheduling control to dynamically adapt wireless resources based on real-time channel conditions, interference patterns, and environmental changes.

Short-Horizon Channel Prediction

Edge AI models that predict localized RF channel fading and interference 5–50 ms ahead to preemptively trigger grant adaptation.

ISAC-Based Obstacle Awareness

Leveraging dual-functional radar-communications waveforms to sense moving cobots, AMRs, and personnel before line-of-sight blockage occurs.

Cycle-Aligned RAN Scheduling

Aligning 5G New Radio (NR) sub-slots and Bandwidth Parts (BWP) with factory automation PLC cycles to eliminate buffering jitter.

2
Wired Core Thrust

Deterministic TSN Core & Zero-Touch Fault Failover

Strengthens the Time-Sensitive Networking (TSN)-based wired core by introducing deterministic end-device transmission support, predictive fault detection, shadow schedule generation, and zero-touch failover mechanisms to ensure bounded latency and near-zero jitter under unexpected network faults.

Commodity NIC Determinism

Hardware-software co-designed transmission mechanisms (e.g., KeepON) that enable standard commercial NICs to achieve microsecond-precise TSN timing.

Predictive Fault Detection

Early detection of cable degradation, buffer overflows, and switch port anomalies through real-time traffic telemetry and TSNKit analytics.

Shadow Schedules & Zero-Touch Failover

Pre-compiled redundant Gate Control Lists (GCLs) ready to instantly swap transmission paths upon fault detection with zero packet drops.

3
Cross-Domain Control Thrust

Cross-Domain Orchestration & Verifiable End-to-End Guarantees

Addresses cross-domain resource management by jointly partitioning, reserving, and scheduling wireless and wired resources to support heterogeneous industrial applications with diverse requirements in timing, throughput, and computation.

Joint Resource Partitioning

Unified mathematical optimization models that dynamically co-allocate 5G radio resource blocks and TSN bandwidth across the shop floor continuum.

Contract-Based End-to-End SLAs

Formalized performance contracts that translate high-level manufacturing safety and cycle-time requirements into verifiable cyber network bounds.

Closed-Loop Adaptation

Tight feedback loops where predictive wireless intelligence triggers preemptive wired adjustments before quality-of-service violations occur.

Track 1: Collaborative Research: VINES: Track 1: NSF-SRC: Towards Resilient and Converged NextG Manufacturing Systems (2549126)

Problem to Solve

A central challenge in NextG manufacturing system (NGMS) is ensuring resilient and deterministic connectivity across wired and wireless domains in dynamic, failure-prone environments.

Existing solutions are largely reactive and siloed, leaving manufacturing systems vulnerable to interference, equipment failures, and unpredictable disruptions.

Vector A

• Resilience: maintain operation despite interference or faults

• Convergence: coordinate across wired/wireless domains with bounded latency and predictable performance

Vector B

• AI-Native Design: ML-driven, short-horizon prediction at the network edge to support timely and reliable adaptation.

• ISAC: Incorporate sensing and communication information into network awareness and coordination.

Project Contributions

Advance the cyber infrastructure design for NGMS by enabling predictive coordination across converged wireless and wired networks, establishing a resilient communications foundation.

Our Approach

• Enhance wireless edge with ML-driven perception to adapt scheduling via ISAC-based environmental awareness.

• Extend the TSN core with hybrid hardware-software mechanisms to preserve determinism under faults.

• Establish a cross-domain control layer to unify predictive wireless edge and deterministic core into verifiable end-to-end guarantees.
